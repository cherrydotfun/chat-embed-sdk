How this example works

This page intentionally has no Phantom code, no wallet adapter, no setWalletAddress call. The iframe is mounted with just appId, roomId, and embedUrl from /config.json.

When you click Connect Wallet inside the chat panel, the iframe drives the entire flow — Phantom popup, signature, Cherry JWT — without round-tripping back to this page.

Use this when your site doesn't have a backend (or you don't want to wire Phantom into it) and you want a "drop-in" chat widget.
